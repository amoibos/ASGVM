# keeps the watch, not the microchip, after GIVE WATCH TO M; here he would carry
# one thing too many at TAKE LETTER. One line differs: DROP MICROCHIP.
# Zorin's study arms a ten-minute alarm (timer 1, record 858) on entry; the
# original catches Bond at the transcript's tenth command there, so the study
# is left sooner: no locked first OPEN DRAWER, no letter, no EXAMINE DESK.
# The gun is on Bond's person; this release wants it drawn before SHOOT ZORIN
# (the original: "You'll have to draw the gun first."), so DRAW GUN comes first. With full
# hands the gun falls, so the calculator (no longer needed) is dropped first.
